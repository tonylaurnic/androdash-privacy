Privacy Policy – AndroDash

Last updated: May 2026

AndroDash respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

Information We Collect

AndroDash may access the following device features and data:

  • Camera access for dash cam video recording
  • Microphone access for audio recording (optional)
  • Location access for GPS tracking and speed information (optional)
  • Storage access to save recorded videos on your device
  • Notification access to display recording status

AndroDash does not sell personal data to third parties.

How We Use Information

The collected data is used only to provide the app’s core functionality, including:

  • Recording driving videos
  • Saving videos locally on the device
  • Displaying GPS/speed information if enabled
  • Managing background recording services

Data Storage

Recorded videos and related data are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly use cloud backup services.

Third-Party Services

AndroDash may use third-party services such as:

  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics / Crash Reporting (if enabled)

These services may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Permissions

AndroDash may request the following permissions:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Location
  • Notifications
  • Foreground service access

Permissions are requested only when required for app functionality.

Children’s Privacy

AndroDash is not directed to children under 13 years old.
